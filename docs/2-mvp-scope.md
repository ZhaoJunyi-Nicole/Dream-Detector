# MVP Scope & Feature Backlog

## The Ultimate Product Vision

DreamLog is an application for people who are curious about their dreams. It helps users record and organize their dreams, remember them, and make their dreams more vivid with AI-generated pictures and melodies.

## The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)

To launch a functional version by **December 25, 2027** focusing strictly on core value.

## Feature Prioritization Matrix

- **Must Have (MVP Lineup):**

  - `Dream Recording`: Users can write down their dreams. This is the main function of the app.
  - `Dream Organization`: Users can view and organize their previous dream records to remember their dreams.
  - `AI Dream Image Generation`: Users can turn their dream descriptions into pictures to make their dreams more vivid.

- **Nice to Have (Post-Launch Backlog):**

  - `Dream Melody Generation`: Users can generate melodies based on their dream descriptions. This feature can be added in a later version.
  - `Dream Continuation`: Users can explore what may happen next in their dreams. This feature can be added after the main functions are completed.
